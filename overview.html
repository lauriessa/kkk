BACKGROUND

https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2024/07/22/2372075/175-million-filipinos-cant-afford-basic-needs-psa


FOR MILLIONS OF FILIPINOS, THE RISING COST OF BASIC FOOD IS MORE THAN JUST A STATISTIC. IT IS A DAILY STRUGGLE.DESPITE NATIONAL PROGRESS, OVER 17 MILLION PEOPLE STILL LIVE BELOW THE POVERTY LINE AND FACE THE CONSTANT THREAT OF PRICE VOLATILITY.

THIS FINANCIAL PRESSURE IS OFTEN EXACERBATED BY EXTERNAL COSTS, SUCH AS FUEL INFLATION. WHEN FUEL PRICES RISE, COMMUTERS ARE FORCED TO SPEND THEIR LIMITED FUNDS ON TRAVEL RATHER THAN ESSENTIAL GOODS, WHILE THE HIGH COST OF MOVING PRODUCTS CREATES DELAYS IN CASH FLOW (Lazaro, 2023).

These logistics challenges do not hit every area equally. Because transport costs depend on distance and infrastructure, food prices vary significantly across different regions. This means that a family’s ability to afford a meal often depends entirely on where they live, leaving those in remote or high-cost areas even more vulnerable to hunger.

OUR MOTIVATION
SUDDEN SPIKES IN FOOD PRICES MAKE ESSENTIAL GOODS HARDER FOR INDIVIDUALS TO AFFORD. DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITIES FEEL THE STRONGEST IMPACT, WHICH CAN DEEPEN EXISTING INEQUALITIES.

OUR STUDY INVESTIGATES THE TIMING AND IMPACT OF FOOD PRICE SPIKES ACROSS THE PHILIPPINES FROM 2021 TO 2025.
THE PROBLEM
FREQUENT FOOD PRICE SPIKES ARE A MAJOR PROBLEM IN THE PHILIPPINES BECAUSE THEY HAPPEN AT DIFFERENT TIMES ACROSS REGIONS, MAKING IT HARDER FOR THE POOR TO RECOVER.
OUR SOLUTION
OUR PROJECT DELIVERS A DEEP-DIVE ANALYSIS INTO HOW PRICE HIKES DIRECTLY IMPACT POVERTY ACROSS THE PHILIPPINES.

BY COMPARING THESE TRENDS WITH HOUSEHOLD INCOMES, WE IDENTIFY GAPS IN THE MARKET AND DEVELOP SMARTER STRATEGIES FOR A MORE STABLE FOOD SYSTEM.
